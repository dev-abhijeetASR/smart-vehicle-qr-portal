⚡ How Privacy Portal Delivery Works

When someone scans your decal, they are taken to a privacy portal where they tap reasons like "Car blocking way". Alerts arrive on your Telegram without revealing your phone number!

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Copy the HTTP API Token (e.g. 7912345678:AAHk...).
  3. Message @userinfobot to get your numerical Chat Id.
  4. Send /start to your bot so it can message you!
📞 Enables "Direct Private Call" on your portal. Finder can call your Telegram handle directly without seeing your phone number.
If hosted on Vercel or GitHub Pages, enter your full URL (e.g., https://my-car.vercel.app/scanner-portal.html).

💬 Zero-Server WhatsApp Direct

No bot or server setup required. When scanned with any smartphone camera, it immediately opens a pre-filled WhatsApp chat to your phone number.

*Note: The person scanning will see your phone number once they open WhatsApp.
